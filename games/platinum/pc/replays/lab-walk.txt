# Boots a save, then walks; which is what the growth systems count.
#
# The day care's step callback runs once per completed step, and everything in
# the growth loop is gated on it: a deposited pair's egg appears when the second
# parent's own counter wraps, and a party egg loses a cycle when the shared
# counter reaches 255. A station sets those counters near their thresholds in
# its recipe, so what is left to walk is a handful of steps, but they have to
# be REAL steps, taken by the player on a real map, or the callback never runs.
#
# Left and right alternately, because one held direction walks into the edge of
# the town and stops stepping.
#
# Every button after the walk is b, and that is the whole trick. Hatching runs
# a script that waits on a button and then a cutscene that ends by asking
# whether to nickname the hatchling, with the cursor sitting on YES, so an A
# there opens the naming screen and the run spends the rest of its frames on a
# keyboard. B answers both: it clears a waiting message like A does, it says no
# to the prompt, and on an idle overworld it does nothing at all.
